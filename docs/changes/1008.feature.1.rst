``AveragedCrossspectrum.raw_coherence`` and
``AveragedCrossspectrum.intrinsic_coherence`` take an optional ``power1_noise`` and
``power2_noise``, so a caller can supply a frequency-dependent Poisson noise level
instead of the flat level of pure counting statistics. The functions underneath
already accepted an array; there was simply no way to reach them. Dead time is the
usual reason the level is neither flat nor equal to 2 in Leahy units, and the intrinsic
coherence subtracts that level, so it was previously biased with no way to fix it.
