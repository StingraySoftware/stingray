``simple_ir`` and ``relativistic_ir``, in ``Simulator`` and in ``stingray.simulator.transfer``, now integrate the impulse response over each time bin, so that its shape and its sum (the gain) no longer depend on ``dt``. ``simple_ir`` has a gain of ``intensity`` and spreads a ``start`` or ``width`` that is not a multiple of ``dt`` over the bins it covers. ``relativistic_ir`` takes a new ``intensity`` argument for its gain, so that ``p1`` and ``p2`` only set its shape, and its ``rise`` and ``decay`` are now in 1 / s, with defaults of 4.8 and 0.8 that keep the previous default shape at ``dt = 0.125``.
