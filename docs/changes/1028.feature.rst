``Simulator.simple_ir`` and ``Simulator.relativistic_ir`` accept ``normalize=True``, which integrates the impulse response over each time bin, so that its sum (the gain) and its shape no longer depend on ``dt``. ``simple_ir`` then has a gain of ``intensity`` and spreads a ``start`` or ``width`` that is not a multiple of ``dt`` over the bins it covers. ``relativistic_ir`` has a gain of 1, with ``rise`` and ``decay`` in units of 1 / s.
