Added ``stingray.crossspectrum.cross_detector_spectral_matrix``, the cross-spectral matrix of two energy bands built by crossing two detectors, and ``stingray.fourier.cross_detector_coherence``, its bias-corrected intrinsic coherence with a 1-sigma interval (``return_interval``). Neither needs a Poisson noise level. With dead time, passing the cross spectra of the two bands within each detector (``same_detector_cross``, also returned by ``cross_detector_spectral_matrix``) keeps the interval's coverage at 68%.
