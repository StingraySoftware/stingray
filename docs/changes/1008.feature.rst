New module ``stingray.deadtime.crosstalk``, for the dead-time coupling between the
energy bands of one detector. Dead time makes the live time a gain that every band
shares, which mixes the bands' cross-spectral matrix by a known real matrix: a steady
source then shows a spurious coherence of several tenths, a real partial coherence is
suppressed by up to a factor of four, and a phase lag is stretched. The new functions
build a cross-spectral matrix that has no Poisson noise level in any entry, the
diagonal included, by crossing two detectors
(``cross_module_cross_spectral_matrix``), and undo the mixing with its inverse
(``deadtime_mixing_matrix``, ``unmix_cross_spectral_matrix``,
``coherence_from_cross_spectral_matrix``). One inversion corrects the band powers, the
coherence and the lag together. ``live_time_fraction`` gives the live time fraction
from the detected rate, which is exact for non-paralyzable dead time.
