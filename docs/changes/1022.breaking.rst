Removed everything deprecated in Stingray 2.0 or earlier:

- the ``lc`` keyword of ``Powerspectrum``, ``AveragedPowerspectrum`` and ``Multitaper``, and the ``lc1``/``lc2`` keywords of ``Crossspectrum`` and ``AveragedCrossspectrum`` (use ``data``, ``data1``, ``data2``);
- the standalone functions ``stingray.coherence``, ``stingray.time_lag``, ``normalize_crossspectrum`` and ``normalize_crossspectrum_gauss`` in ``stingray.crossspectrum`` (use the methods of ``AveragedCrossspectrum`` and the functions in ``stingray.fourier``);
- ``stingray.stats.classical_pvalue`` (use ``pds_probability``), ``stingray.gti.gti_len`` (use ``get_total_gti_length``), and ``rms_calculation``, ``avg_pds_from_events`` and ``avg_cs_from_events`` in ``stingray.fourier`` (use ``get_rms_from_unnorm_periodogram`` and ``avg_cs_from_timeseries``/``avg_pds_from_timeseries``);
- ``Lightcurve.estimate_chunk_length`` and ``Lightcurve.analyze_lc_chunks`` (use ``estimate_segment_size`` and ``analyze_segments``), and the ``axis`` keyword of ``Lightcurve.plot`` (use ``axis_limits``);
- the ``ncounts`` keyword of ``EventList`` and the ``bin_time`` keyword of ``EventList.simulate_times``;
- the ``norm`` keyword of ``stingray.pulse.z_n`` (use ``datatype="binned"``) and the ``threads`` keyword of the ``sample`` methods in ``stingray.modeling`` (use ``pool``).

Where a removed keyword sat in the middle of a signature, the keywords after it can now only be passed by name, so that old positional calls fail with an error instead of silently filling the wrong argument.

Every module now declares its public names in ``__all__``. ``import stingray`` now gives direct access only to the main classes (``EventList``, ``Lightcurve``, ``Powerspectrum``, ``AveragedCrossspectrum``, ``Bispectrum``, ``RmsSpectrum``, ...), imported one by one instead of with ``from ... import *``. Functions must be imported from their modules: for example, ``stingray.pds_probability`` is now ``stingray.stats.pds_probability``, and ``stingray.rebin_data`` is now ``stingray.utils.rebin_data``. The documentation of ``stingray.io`` only lists the functions meant for users.
